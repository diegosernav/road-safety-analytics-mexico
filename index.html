Road Safety Analytics — Mexico

An academic data analytics project examining road traffic accidents across Mexico, with a focus on Nuevo León and the Monterrey Metropolitan Area. Using public INEGI data, the project combines descriptive, temporal, demographic, geographic, geospatial, and predictive analysis to identify patterns and support data-driven road safety decisions.

Language: Project content and visualizations are presented in Spanish.
